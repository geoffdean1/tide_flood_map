Please read

This is a planning aid, not an official forecast. Check the street where you park.

  • Ground heights come from a lidar model and can be off by a few inches. Treat anything within a foot as uncertain.
  • With "Barriers hold" selected, water spreads from the harbor only over ground lower than the water level, so walled-off low spots get no color. The map is built from cells about 1 to 2 meters wide, so a thin wall or a narrow gap can be missed either way. No color does not mean safe. Choose "Barriers fail or are bypassed" to see everything that is low.
  • Walls you draw or load under "Known walls" are your assumptions, not measurements. A wall set too high can hide real flooding, so say so when you share a result.
  • Storm drains, tidal valves, pumps, sandbags and berms are not included. Drain backup can flood streets below the harbor level.
  • The 22-inch figure is the City's illustration. Actual water has run 18 to 22 inches or more above forecast.
